# Public records requests — assessment rolls, five counties

**1,076 auction parcels** in these five counties have no valuation and no free
online route to one. Every automated avenue has been checked and closed: no
statewide roll exists in either state, the counties publish per-parcel lookups
rather than files, and the one county that does publish a CSV serves HTTP 403 to
any request including for `robots.txt`.

What remains is asking. These are public records in both states.

## When a file arrives

The pipeline expects a parcel identifier and a value. Getting one of these back
is not a manual job — `lib/property/providers/cadastre.ts` already maps arbitrary
column names onto our fields, and the same shape works for a CSV.

The check that matters is the one `verify-parcel-layer.ts` performs on a GIS
layer: do **our** parcel numbers appear in it. A roll for the right county whose
identifiers do not match our sale list is the same dead end as a wrong-county
layer, and it is worth confirming on five rows before loading 73,000.

## Contacts, and how they were obtained

Every address, phone number and email above was read from an official county page
during research on 14 August 2026, with the source URL recorded. **No email

letter says to use the portal or post instead. Franklin County MO and San Juan
County NM publish no assessor email; both are routed accordingly.
