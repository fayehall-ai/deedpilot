Executive Auction Description

The Washington County tax sale scheduled for Wednesday, November 4, 2026 is an online auction for property subject to delinquent property-tax execution. The county sells a certificate representing the unpaid tax debt, not the property itself. The owner can redeem by paying the debt plus statutory interest; if they don't, the certificate holder can eventually apply for a deed. Under Colorado Revised Statutes Title 39, Art. 11, redemption runs 3 years from sale, and the return to a purchaser whose position is redeemed is 9 points above federal discount rate, set annually; premium/bid-down format varies by county. I. Statutory Framework

Colorado conducts this sale as a tax lien under Colorado Revised Statutes Title 39, Art. 11. The county sells a certificate representing the unpaid tax debt, not the property itself. Note on provenance. The sale type, redemption, returns, statute, notes above are Colorado's statewide rule rather than something separately researched for Washington County. That is ordinarily the right answer — a statute is the state's — but counties differ in how they apply it, so confirm the mechanics on the county's own sale notice.

II. Redemption and the Purchaser's Return

Redemption: 3 years from sale.

Return if redeemed: 9 points above federal discount rate, set annually; premium/bid-down format varies by county.

A lien purchase is a debt position, not a property position. The return above is what the debt pays if it is redeemed; taking title is a later, separate and uncertain process.

VI. Before You Bid

Everything below must be answered by you, from the county and from the ground.

  • Confirm the sale date, start time and venue on the county's own notice — not on a calendar.
  • Confirm registration, deposit amount, accepted funds and the payment deadline in writing.
  • Order a title and lien search on every parcel you intend to bid.
  • Ask the county which municipal and governmental liens it treats as surviving the sale.
  • Confirm occupancy and access on the ground; a deed does not deliver possession.
  • Inspect physical condition yourself — the sale list is not a condition report.
  • Price the curative step (quiet title or barment) into the bid, in months and in fees.
  • Decide the exit before bidding, and the walk-away number with it.
